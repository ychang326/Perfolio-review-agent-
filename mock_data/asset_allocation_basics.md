# Asset Allocation Basics

Asset allocation is the process of dividing a portfolio among asset classes such as equities, fixed income, funds, and cash.

## Why it matters
- Diversification can reduce idiosyncratic risk.
- Target weights should reflect risk tolerance, time horizon, and liquidity needs.
- Drift happens when markets move; rebalancing brings weights back to policy.

## Common views
- By **asset class** (Equity / Fixed Income / Cash / Fund)
- By **sector** (Technology, Financials, Government, etc.)
- By **currency** or geography when relevant

## How to use this assistant
Ask for your live holdings and weights. The assistant reports facts from portfolio tools and does not provide personalized buy/sell advice.
