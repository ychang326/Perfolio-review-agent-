# Concentration Risk Guide

Concentration risk is the risk of large losses because portfolio value depends heavily on a small set of exposures.

## Types
1. **Single-name concentration** — one ticker dominates portfolio weight.
2. **Sector concentration** — many holdings share the same industry driver.
3. **Asset-class concentration** — almost all capital in one asset class (e.g. 100% equity).
4. **Factor concentration** — shared sensitivity (rates, growth, FX).

## Practical checks
- List top weights and sum of top 3 / top 5.
- Compare sector weights vs a policy or blended benchmark when available.
- Review pending sells/buys that may change concentration soon.
