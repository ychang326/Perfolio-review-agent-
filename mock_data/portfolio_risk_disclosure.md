# Portfolio Risk Disclosure (Educational)

This document is for general education. It is not a solicitation or investment advice.

## Key risk concepts
- **Market risk**: prices can fall as well as rise.
- **Volatility**: how much returns fluctuate over time.
- **Drawdown**: peak-to-trough decline in portfolio value.
- **Concentration**: large exposure to one issuer, sector, or theme.
- **Liquidity**: some instruments may be harder to exit quickly.
- **Currency risk**: FX moves affect non-base-currency holdings.

## Diagnosis labels used by tools
When position risk tools run, they may return:
- `RISK_NORMAL` — metrics within typical watch bands
- `RISK_WATCH` — elevated weight or volatility proxy
- `RISK_ELEVATED` — high concentration and/or high volatility proxy

## Important
Past performance is not indicative of future results. Always read product factsheets and disclosures for specific instruments.
